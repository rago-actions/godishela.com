A Guide to Working with Me

How I learn, work, and take feedback, so we can skip the guesswork. First written in June 2022.

I learn fast top-down, outside-in

I absorb information best when I understand the context, the history, the big picture, the ‘what’ and the ‘why’, before going into details and the ‘how’.

I'm driven by frequent, bite-sized milestones

I deliver best when I work towards SMART goals with regular milestones for feedback and course correction.

I enjoy a mix of deep work and meetings

Deep work gives me thrill and satisfaction. Time with people keeps me grounded and self-aware. A good day for me has separate blocks of both. I'm most effective in meetings when I know what's expected of me, so I can come in prepared.

I thrive on respect

Inconsideration, aggression, blame and condescension, especially in group settings, affect me negatively, even when meant as a joke.

I get excited when two different things connect

The best, most memorable piece of feedback I ever got compared my working style to binary tree traversal algorithms.

I take feedback best in structured, private conversations

A little notice beforehand helps me prepare to receive it well.

I “look” serious during focused work

Sometimes to the point that I might seem angry or annoyed. I'm really not, and it matters to me that the people I work with know that :) I sometimes keep my camera off to avoid the misunderstanding.

I give trust until it's broken

If you're different and prefer trust to be earned, I'd really appreciate an open conversation about how I can earn yours.
